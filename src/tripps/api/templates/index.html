      Mina periodkort

        Covered legs (e.g. Öresundståg within Skåne with a Skånetrafiken card) price at 0. Assumes an all-zone period ticket; SJ is never auto-freed. A partial-zone ticket is registered but priced as paid (its exact zones can't be verified from the data).

        Tickital-hyror

          A tickital rental is a second-hand period ticket: covered legs price at 0 within its date window. Renting a period ticket violates the operator's terms and the ticket can be blocked; its period cost is not added to the trip total, so it only beats singles across enough covered travel. Registered by hand — tickital has no public listings.
